Watch the CRT and press Enter (or the TAP pad) in rhythm, anticipating each flash — not as a
          reaction after seeing it. 16–32 taps. A USB keyboard on the Pi is more precise than this browser

Software versions

      

      

      

        
        
        
        
      

      

      
How it works: a Pi with internet (Ethernet, phone hotspot at home…) fetches the new version from
        GitHub; then roll out copies it inside the offline cluster to all other Pis, one after the other
        (leader last). Each Pi checks the download, installs, restarts its services (~10 s, the CRT keeps showing its
        animation) and rolls back by itself if it does not come up healthy. Avoid rollouts during the show.
